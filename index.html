          Study Guide

          Quick-reference cheat sheets for the written test, and a full walkthrough for the behind-the-wheel road test.

          Written test cheat sheets

          Sign shapes & colors
          • Red octagon — STOP (the only 8-sided sign)
          • Red inverted triangle — YIELD
          • Red circle, white bar — DO NOT ENTER · red rectangle — WRONG WAY
          • Yellow diamond — warning ahead (curve, merge, animals…)
          • Orange diamond — temporary: work zone (fines often doubled)
          • Yellow pennant — start of NO PASSING ZONE
          • Yellow circle — railroad crossing ahead
          • Yellow-green pentagon — school zone / school crossing
          • White rectangle/square — regulation: speed limit, one way, no parking
          • Green — guidance: directions and exits · Blue — services · Brown — recreation
          Pavement markings
          • Yellow lines separate opposing traffic (left edge on one-way roads); white lines separate same-direction traffic
          • Broken line on your side — passing/lane change allowed when safe
          • Solid line on your side — don't cross to pass
          • Double solid yellow — no passing either direction (cross only for left turns where permitted)
          • Broken-inside yellow center lane — shared two-way left-turn lane only
          • Thick white line — stop line: stop fully before it
          • Painted diamond — HOV lane · bike symbol + white line — bike lane · diagonal stripes — gore, never drive there
          Right of way in 8 lines
          • 4-way stop / simultaneous arrival: first to arrive, then the car on the right
          • Plain green left turn: yield to oncoming traffic and pedestrians (green arrow protects you)
          • Entering from driveway/alley/parking: yield to everyone, sidewalk first
          • T-intersection: the through road goes first
          • Roadway > merging ramp: highway traffic keeps the right of way
          • Emergency vehicle with lights/siren: pull right and stop
          • School bus flashing red, undivided road: everyone stops — both directions
          • Pedestrians in any crosswalk, blind pedestrians anywhere: always yield
          What to do when…
          • Skid — ease off the gas, steer smoothly where you want to go, no hard braking
          • Hydroplane — off the gas, wheel straight, no brakes until tires grip
          • Blowout — grip the wheel, ease off the gas, brake gently once slow
          • Brake failure — pump, downshift, then parking brake gradually; steer to a safe stop
          • Stuck accelerator — shift to NEUTRAL, brake, pull over, then shut off
          • Blinded by high beams — look at the right edge of your lane, slow down
          • Deer ahead — brake in your lane, don't swerve at speed
          • Breakdown on a highway — pull far off, hazards on, stay belted inside
          Parking on hills
          • Downhill + curb — wheels toward the curb (right)
          • Uphill + curb — wheels away from the curb (left), let it roll back a touch to touch the curb
          • No curb — wheels toward the edge of the road, both directions
          • Always: parking brake on, manual in gear (or Park)
          Numbers to remember
          • BAC limit: 0.08% (21+) · 0.04% commercial · ~0.01–0.02% under 21
          • Following distance: 3 seconds (4+ rain, 5–6+ snow/night)
          • Signal before turning: 100 ft (300+ on highways)
          • Dim high beams: 500 ft oncoming, 300 ft following
          • Passing a bicycle: 3 ft minimum
          • Fire hydrant parking: 15 ft · parallel park within 12–18 in of the curb
          • Headlights: sunset to sunrise and when wipers run
          • One standard drink per hour is all the liver clears — time is the only cure

          Road test (behind the wheel)

          Before you go — checklist
          • Permit/license documents, test appointment confirmation
          • Car with current registration + proof of insurance
          • Car in test-ready shape: all lights work, tires healthy, horn, wipers, no warning lights, clean windshield
          • Licensed adult driver accompanies you (required in most states)
          • Arrive early; adjust seat, mirrors, and wheel BEFORE the examiner gets in
          How you're scored
          • Minor errors (rolling a stop slightly, sloppy lane position) subtract points — you can still pass with a few
          • Automatic fails: collision, running a red/stop sign, dangerous action, speeding, examiner grabbing the wheel or brake, mounting a curb, driving the wrong way, failing to yield when someone has to swerve or brake hard for you
          • Examiners watch: scanning (eyes moving, head checks), smooth control, space management, confidence without aggression
          The maneuvers, step by step
          • Parallel parking: signal · pull alongside (about 2–3 ft out) · reverse until your rear seat passes their bumper · turn the wheel hard toward the curb · when at ~45°, straighten, then turn away to bring the front in · center the car, wheels straight, within 12–18 in
          • Three-point turn: signal right, pull to the right curb · check all around · turn hard left and cross to the other side, stopping before the far curb · reverse straight back while turning hard right · then drive forward into the right lane
          • Lane change (SMOG): Signal · Mirrors · Over-the-shoulder glance · Go — smoothly, no drifting, cancel the signal
          • Straight-line backing: hand at 12, look over your right shoulder, creep slowly, correct early and gently
          • Turns: signal 100 ft out, brake before the turn, 9-and-3 push-pull steering, end in the correct lane, gentle acceleration out
          • Stops: full stop behind the line, scan left-right-left, creep for sightlines when blocked, then commit
          • Hill parking: see the cheat sheet above — curb direction is a classic scored item
          Most common fails & how to avoid them
          • Rolling stops — count "one-one-thousand" at every stop sign
          • No head checks — exaggerate them; if the examiner can't SEE you checking, it didn't happen
          • Speeding in the test zone or parking lot — parking lots and school zones count; drive 5 under when unsure
          • Hesitating too long — waiting forever to merge or turn fails too; take the safe gap decisively
          • One-handed driving / phone touch — both hands, phone away, radio off
          • Curb strike on maneuvers — slow is passable, touching the curb often isn't
          • Improper lane / no signal — get in the turn lane early, signal everything, always
          Day-of tips
          • Sleep, eat, and practice in the SAME car you test in
          • Smoothness beats speed — examiners pass calm drivers
          • Make one mistake? Let it go. They score the whole drive, not your worst second
          • Narrate light head checks and mirror scans — visibility earns credit
          • Don't argue with instructions; if unclear, ask them to repeat
          • Fail? Most people pass the second attempt — book it and drill your weak maneuver
